How to play

← → turn the barrel, ↑ ↓ set power, Q E switch weapon, A D drive. Hold Shift for bigger steps.

Space arms the shot and shows what you are about to fire. Space again confirms, Esc cancels.

You can also drag from your tank, or from the ring at the end of the aim line. Clicking elsewhere on the battlefield does nothing.

Every tank opens with a free tracer: five harmless shots on your aim and at 5° and 10° above and below it. Each plants a flag marked with its offset, so you can read off the correction. After that, the dotted arc shows where your last shot went.
