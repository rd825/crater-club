How to play

← → turn the barrel, ↑ ↓ set power, Q E switch weapon, A D drive. Hold Shift for bigger steps.

Each turn has two steps. First shape the ground if you want to: Z digs a tunnel along your barrel, X burrows into a foxhole, C builds a wall, V lays a bridge. Then fire.

The ground is real material. Tunnels and overhangs hold, loose dirt pours and piles up, and water floods low ground and floats tanks. A tank that ends its own turn sitting in lava burns, so drive, dig or bridge your way out first. Water turns lava to stone.

Space arms the shot and shows what you are about to fire. Space again confirms, Esc cancels.

You can also drag from your tank, or from the ring at the end of the aim line. Clicking elsewhere on the battlefield does nothing.

Every tank opens with a free tracer: five harmless shots on your aim and at 5° and 10° above and below it. Each plants a flag marked with its offset, so you can read off the correction. After that, the dotted arc shows where your last shot went.

The playground gives you every weapon with nothing running out, targets that never shoot back, and free digging and driving. W opens the full weapon list there.
